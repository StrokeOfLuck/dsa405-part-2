What are we looking at?

The PDF lists transactions made by the member or a reported owner. One filing can contain many transaction rows.

What the parser needs

For each row, it must separate an asset, transaction type, trade date, and disclosed amount range. The PDF was made for people to read; copying its text is much messier.
